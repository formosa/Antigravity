Card Display

Cards have a standardized Active Display Colors, and unique Inactive Display Colors that are based on the File Type the card is associated with.

Active Display Colors

Top Bar (active): (border-color: #5A5FEB), (background-color: #E0E7FF), (color: #4338CA)
Center Content (active): (background-color: #FFFFFF) (color: #111827)
Bottom Bar (active): (background-color: #F9FAFB), (color: #111827)

Standardized appearance for all cards when active/focused.
